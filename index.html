How it works

Three steps to maths confidence

Mathic makes it easy for parents to get started and easy for children to keep going.

Create a profile in under a minute. Mathic loads the exact topics your child is covering at school right now.

Islands

A year of maths, one island at a time.

Every island is one topic from the UK National Curriculum for your child's school year: place value, fractions, times tables, shape and more. Years 1 to 6 have 78 islands in all.

📅

Follows the school year

Islands run in the order most schools teach them, from September to July. Each time your child opens Mathic, the map starts on this week's island, so practice at home matches what's happening in class.

🧭

Free to explore

Your child can still tap any island in their year, to go back over a tricky topic or race ahead to the next one.

🏆

Finish an island

Passing an island's Boss Round marks the topic as finished, so you can see at a glance what they've mastered.

Ways to play

Five ways to play every island.

Each island has five modes, from gentle practice to a school-style check-up. Your child picks; each one builds a different skill.

📚

Practise

Free

A balanced set of arithmetic, reasoning and problem-solving questions, with instant feedback and a hint after a wrong answer. Builds fluency and confidence at their own pace.

⚡

Challenge

3 acorns

Twice as many hard questions as Practise. Stretches confident learners and keeps strong mathematicians interested.

🎲

Mix

2 acorns

Questions from the islands they've already finished, all mixed together. Keeps earlier topics fresh, so nothing is forgotten by the end of term.

🏆

Boss Round

10 acorns

The island's big test, with more problem solving. Pass it to finish the island and show they've cracked the topic.

📝

Assessment

Free

A short check-up in the style of a school test: no answers shown until the end, then a full review with stars. Gets them ready for real tests, calmly.

🌰 Acorns are earned in the Daily Quest, never bought, so the bonus modes are a reward for regular practice.

Safety & privacy

Made for children. Controlled by parents.

Mathic is built to the UK Children's Code. Here is what that means in practice.

🚫

No ads, ever

Mathic never shows adverts, whether on the Free plan or Premium.

💬

No chat, no contact

Children can't message anyone, and nobody can message them. There are no open text boxes for children.

🔒

A grown-up check

Purchases, settings and links that leave the app sit behind a quick check that only a grown-up can pass.

📊

Anonymous analytics

We count which screens and modes are used, without knowing who used them: no names, no device IDs and no location.

👀

You're in control

Only you can create the account and add children. You can see each child's progress and delete everything from Settings at any time.

🇬🇧

UK GDPR and the ICO

Mathic is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ZC285429). Our Privacy Policy explains exactly what we keep and why.

Mathic is made by Sam, a London dad. When his son's maths homework moved on to a new topic, the practice at home didn't. So he built an app that follows the school year, topic by topic.

Questions

How does the free trial work?

Your 7-day free trial starts when you create your account and unlocks every topic in your child's school year. You don't need payment details, and you're never charged automatically. After 7 days your account moves to the Free plan: your child keeps all their progress and can keep playing the first two topics of their school year. To unlock every topic again, upgrade to Premium in Settings.

Does Mathic follow what my child learns at school?

Yes. Mathic follows the UK National Curriculum, topic by topic, in the order most schools teach it through the year.

Is it safe for my child?

There are no ads, no chat and no way for children to contact anyone. Only you can create the account, and purchases are protected behind a parent check.

I have more than one child. Does one subscription cover them all?

Yes. One Premium subscription covers up to 5 children. Each child has their own profile and progress, and plays every topic in their own school year. Your children can be in the same year or in different years, in any mix from Year 1 to Year 6. The Free plan includes one child.

Is Mathic available on Android?

Not yet. Mathic is on iPhone and iPad for now.
